## Reviewed and found sound — no change made

- **`ControlFrame` widening is safe against the authored envelopes.** 696
  replaced `MessageRouter.response_message?/1`'s narrow `%{"id" => _, "result"
  => _}` with `ControlFrame.classify/1`, which now treats *any* top-level
  `"result"`, any non-nil `"error"`, and `ping`/`pong` under `method`/`op`/
  `event`/`type` as transport control. I checked every venue's
  `discriminator_field` in `lib/bourse/ws/envelope.ex` (`e`, `topic`,
  `params.channel`, `channel`, `arg.channel`) plus binance spot's shape-matched
  partial-book snapshot (`lastUpdateId`/`bids`/`asks`): none carries a top-level
  `result` or a non-nil `error`, and derive's `{"method":"subscription"}` is
  matched by the explicit market clause ahead of the control clauses. No venue's
  market data is reclassified.
- **The reviewer commits are real fixes, not rubber stamps.** 9475f0b narrowed a
  module-wide `rescue` on `OrderOptions.call/5` down to `request_shapes/4`, so
  only shaping's caller-input `invalid_parameters` is converted and a genuine
  error from `Unified.call/5` can no longer be swallowed past its own
  raise-vs-tuple contract; it also added the missing numeric guard on trigger and
  protective prices. b4daa6f made the Deribit trade `fee`/`fees` `omit_if_empty`
  (the same field map serves fee-less public trades) and replaced a literal
  `contract_size: 0.001` with an assertion against the venue's own value, after
  deribit re-scaled `ETH_USDC-PERPETUAL` to `0.0001`. 8304acb fixed the
  reservation to the waiter's own cost and persisted the whole call's delay
  rather than one bucket's, and moved two `# Client API` / `# Server callbacks`
  section comments the implementer had left in the wrong place.
- **No leftover debug, no skip tags, no hidden-failure patterns** in the range.
  The single `IO.inspect` is the reporting function of `scripts/probe_ws_heartbeat.exs`,
  a header-documented diagnostic sitting beside the existing
  `scripts/rotate_authored_specs.py`; that is its output, not a leftover.
- **Two new `Process.sleep` calls in `test/bourse/rate_limiter_test.exs`** (500 ms
  and 20 ms) are not flake-papering: the limiter reads
  `System.monotonic_time/1` directly with no injectable clock, so reservation
  expiry cannot be tested without real elapsed time. The 500 ms wait clears a
  ~375 ms reservation deadline — a ~125 ms margin, which is tight under load but
  is a consequence of the module's design, not of this diff. Left alone; an
  injectable clock is a design change, not audit scope.
