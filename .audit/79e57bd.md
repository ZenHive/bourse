### 5. A live contract graded venue drift at `1.0e-12` — FIXED

Surfaced by the cold check as a `flaky` result, which is how a latent live-lane
flake stays invisible.
`test/live/bybit/bybit_authored_integration_test.exs:411` read `fundingRate` from
`GET /v5/market/tickers`, called `Bourse.fetch_funding_rate/2`, and asserted the
two agreed within `1.0e-12`. That field is the **predicted** next funding rate and
moves continuously: the run saw `1.3943e-4` then `1.4487e-4` seconds apart, five
orders of magnitude above the tolerance. The test now takes a second raw sample
after the unified call and asserts the unified value falls within `[min, max]` of
the two observations — a wrong field or a scale error still fails hard, the
venue's own drift no longer can. Re-run green (`:411` — 1 passed). Recorded in
`BUGS.md`.
